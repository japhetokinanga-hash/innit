USSD and SMS: for a basic phone

A farmer does not need a smartphone to use ShambaPulse. The USSD menu works in English and Kiswahili, and alerts arrive as ordinary SMS messages.

The five-option menu

The menu offers five choices, in both languages:

  1. Weather forecast
  2. Market prices
  3. Advisories
  4. Subscribe to alerts
  5. Opt out of alerts

Registration happens through option 4, Subscribe to alerts: a new farmer chooses a ward and a language, then answers the Data Protection Act 2019 consent prompt. If consent is declined, nothing is stored. A farmer's home ward is the default, and further wards can be added.

Simulated SMS in a demonstration is a demo outcome, not a fallback for a failed delivery. In the live system, a failed delivery is recorded as failed, not hidden.

How to subscribe

  1. Dial the code (*384#)

    Dial the ShambaPulse USSD code (*384#) from any phone.

  2. Choose option 4

    Select "Subscribe to alerts" from the menu.

  3. Pick ward and language

    Choose your home county and whether you want English or Kiswahili.

  4. Give consent

    Answer the Data Protection Act 2019 consent prompt. If you decline, nothing is stored.

What you can do

  • Weather

    Look up the forecast for a ward, in English or Kiswahili.

  • Check

    Look up prices and advisories without waiting for an alert, and subscribe through option 4 with consent recorded.

  • Control

    Subscribe to a ward, or opt out, at any time from the same menu.

Common questions

Does it cost money to use USSD?

Charges depend on your network and the final service setup. This answer will be confirmed when the service goes live.

What if I change my mind about consent?

Choose option 5, "Opt out of alerts", from the same menu at any time.

Can I follow more than one ward?

Yes. Your home ward is the default, and further wards can be added.
