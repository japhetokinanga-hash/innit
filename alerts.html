Alerts

Alerts carry county and regional advisories to farmers in a specific county. They are kept short so each one fits one SMS message, and they name the region rather than pretending to be a ward forecast.

How an alert is sent

  1. An officer creates an alert for a County, in English and Kiswahili.
  2. The system finds farmers in that county who have consented and subscribed.
  3. If no farmer is eligible, the alert stays a draft - it is not marked as sent.
  4. Delivery outcomes are recorded, so the officer can see the totals for that alert and filter them by ward.

Separating "submitted" from "confirmed delivered" matters: a message that was sent is not the same as a message that arrived.

Example topics

  • Short-rains onset and planting advice

  • Animal disease and crop disease warnings

What an alert looks like

Every alert is written in both languages. These are sample messages, short enough to fit a single SMS.

English

ShambaPulse: Heavy rain expected in Kisii tomorrow. Avoid crossing rivers and stay indoors during lightning.

Kiswahili

ShambaPulse: Mvua kubwa inatarajiwa Kisii kesho. Epuka kuvuka mito na ukae ndani wakati wa radi.

Types of alert

  • Weather

    Rain, flood and storm warnings, with practical safety advice.

  • Advisory

    Seasonal guidance: planting, certified seed, drainage and animal care.

  • Market

    Price movements and market notices for the farmer's ward.

When you receive an alert

  1. Read it fully

    Check the ward, the date and the advice before acting.

  2. Act early

    Move animals, protect stored harvest and plan field work around the warning.

  3. Pass it on

    Tell neighbours who may not have a phone or may have missed the message.

Common questions

Will I get alerts for every ward?

You receive alerts for your home county by default. You can add further wards from the USSD menu.

Can I stop alerts?

Yes. Choose "Opt out of alerts" in the USSD menu at any time.

Why did I not get an alert?

Alerts only go to farmers in the county who have consented and subscribed. Check your subscription from the USSD menu.

Not receiving alerts yet?

Subscribe from any phone, no internet needed.
