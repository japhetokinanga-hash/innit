Our Story

Rooted in Kenyan soil

ShambaPulse started with a simple observation: many farmers still make big decisions without timely information. Weather changes, market prices and pest outbreaks move fast, and a farmer who hears too late can lose a whole season.

We are a team of students and developers in Strathmore University, Nairobi, building a platform that puts that information in every farmer's hands, whether through a smartphone or a basic phone using USSD.

Our Mission

Information that reaches the farm

To help farmers increase their harvests and income by giving them reliable, timely and easy-to-use farming information.

We believe good decisions grow good harvests, and every farmer deserves the information to make them.

What We Offer

  • Farm Alerts

    Weather warnings, pest outbreak notices and planting reminders sent when they matter.

  • Market Prices

    Up-to-date prices for maize, beans, vegetables and more, so you can sell at the right time.

  • USSD Access

    No smartphone or internet needed. Dial a short code from any phone to get farm information.

  • Farm Records

    Track crops, expenses and harvests in one place and see how your farm is performing.
